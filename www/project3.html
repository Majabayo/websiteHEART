Klubhouse is a project created in collaboration with Jeanne Mora, and is knitted sextoy plushies, that are created by combining knitwear techniques and 3D software. The product is a huggable and soft version of sextoys sold by an online sextoy retailer. The plushies are meant to be an approachable, fun and playful way of relating to toys that otherwise carry a lot of stigma. In this project they are presented as part of a pop-up store for the brand, a Klubhouse, that is meant to engage people with the body, and exist as a space for open conversation.

▸

The project started with an exploration on how you can translate meshes created with photogrammetry, in to physical objects. and how can the 3D software shape and aesthetics be expressed. Here the medium of knitwear is used to translate the meshes into into a textile.

▸

The meshes are made using photogrammetry software

▸

▸

From scan, to UV map used to create a flat pattern for physical object

▸

using the UV mapping feature in blender, the photogrammetry meshes can be flattened into complex and intricate flatpatterns, that when sewn (or in these trials, taped) together recreate the shape in the physical sphere, but maintain the language of the digital realm.

knitting samples

▸

Using a double bed knitting machine, a variation of textures and patterns were used, in order to recreate the look of the digital meshes, translated into IRL.
