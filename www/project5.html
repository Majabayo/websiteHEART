The missing tapestry (august 2023 - )

A project with studio Bodybuiling. The project is creating an interpretation of a series of tapestries designed for Henry III van Nassau, a decendant of the dutch royal family, in 1531. All the original tapestries have been lost, but 7 out of 8 of the cartoons have been found. But what does the 8th look like? The tapstry will be a 1.5 x 2 m tapestry woven jaquard woven at EE labels. In this project my role has been first developing my own personal research, but as of now overseeing storytelling and final concept development.

my own research has been focoused on the wevers themselves, and on the tapestry guilds in 1500s. Here i found that many of the tapestry makes at the time got prosecuted for retouching (painting in details) in their tapestries. This interfered deeply with the industry and its image of "makers of quality"
