The digital beyond is a soundscape based on research around how we can treat our digital belongings (personal data) with our own death in mind. How much emotional value do assign to our data? And can the pratices of death care provide a lense for us to deal with our digital belongings in a more intensional and considerate way?

The soundscape consists of two interviews, along with a collection of my own data, that all exists online, dating back from 2009 to now, and acts as the backdrop for the soundscape. Below a tracklist is provided:
